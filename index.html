CRIC ON PRO

Interactive demo of the iOS / Android app. It runs the same scoring engine as the backend, with fictional Lahore teams.
📱 Sign in: any 3XX number with OTP 123456, or tap a role (Spectator, Scorer…).
🏏 Score a match: sign in as Scorer. Continue the live match, or start the Qualifier from the toss.
📶 Offline test: in the scorer console tap the cloud chip to go offline, score balls, then go back online and watch them sync.
🌐 Profile → اردو for Urdu (right-to-left) and 🌙 for dark mode.
